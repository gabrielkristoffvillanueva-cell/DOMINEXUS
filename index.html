ABOUT THE SYSTEM

What is DOMINEXUS?

DOMINEXUS is an Offline General Meeting Attendance Management System developed for student organizations of St. Dominic College of Asia.

The system aims to provide a more organized and convenient way of managing attendance during general meetings. Instead of relying entirely on manual attendance sheets, DOMINEXUS allows attendance information to be recorded, organized, and monitored digitally.

Each registered student can have a permanent Unique ID connected to their personal QR code, which can be used during organization meetings.

Purpose

To provide student organizations with a simple, organized, and accessible system for managing general meeting attendance and participation.
