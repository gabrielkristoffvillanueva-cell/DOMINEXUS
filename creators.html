ABOUT THE CREATORS

Built by Students, for Student Organizations

DOMINEXUS was developed as a research and system development project by students of St. Dominic College of Asia.

The project was created to explore a more organized and convenient way of managing attendance during student organization meetings.

OUR WORK

More Than Just a Website

DOMINEXUS represents the team's effort to combine research, technology, and student organization needs.

01

Research

Identifying problems in traditional attendance management and developing a system-based solution.

02

Design

Creating a simple and accessible interface for students and organization personnel.

Development

Building the DOMINEXUS system and its attendance management features.
